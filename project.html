WORLD CUP

FIFA Sound celebrates the culture, creativity and energy surrounding the world’s biggest sporting event like never before. Uniting Canada, Mexico and the United States for the first FIFA World Cup™ ever hosted across three nations, FIFA Sound invite the world to engage with the tournament through a collection of sounds that mirror the beautiful game itself: different styles, paces and souls working in harmony. At the heart of the programme is the Official FIFA World Cup 2026™ Album, with a collective of artists spanning different continents and music genres to align with the ambition and cultural impact of this historic competition. Alongside the Official Album, the FIFA World Cup(tm) Sonic ID provides a unique audio brand to the tournament, unifying the experience of global football fans as well as Host Cities’ respective communities in a memorable way.
